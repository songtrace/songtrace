# SongTrace Vision

SongTrace is an evidence-driven intelligence platform for understanding why music performance changes and what actions are reasonably supported by the available evidence.

Analytics produce measurements. SongTrace produces understanding by moving through a traceable reasoning model:

```text
Evidence -> Observation -> Conclusion -> Recommendation
```

The canonical product vision lives in [`docs/vision/PRODUCT_VISION.md`](vision/PRODUCT_VISION.md).

## Core principles

- Evidence before interpretation.
- Every conclusion must be explainable and traceable.
- Provider neutrality is required.
- Deterministic reasoning should come before AI-assisted reasoning.
- AI should reason over structured SongTrace evidence, observations, conclusions, confidence, and provenance—not raw provider data.
