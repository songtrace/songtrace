# SongTrace Founding Principles

SongTrace exists to help music professionals understand why performance changes, not merely report that it changed.

## Principles

1. **Evidence-first design**
   - Treat provider data, exports, statements, and user records as evidence.
   - Do not let provider-specific schemas leak into the reasoning model.

2. **Traceability**
   - Observations must reference supporting evidence.
   - Conclusions must reference supporting observations.
   - Future recommendations should remain traceable to evidence-backed reasoning.

3. **Provider neutrality**
   - SongTrace should be the intelligence layer above the connected music ecosystem, not a replacement for existing analytics providers.
   - Users should be able to bring their own data from services and subscriptions they already use.

4. **Deterministic reasoning first**
   - Prefer explicit rules and testable extraction before AI-assisted synthesis.
   - Keep the system explainable and reproducible.

5. **AI as assistant, not authority**
   - AI may summarize, critique, and propose hypotheses from structured SongTrace objects.
   - AI must not create evidence or make unsupported factual claims.

6. **Private data stays private**
   - Real-world validation files should remain local unless explicitly sanitized and approved for inclusion.
